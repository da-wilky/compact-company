# Simple books are a view over the journal, not a second ledger

A small business filing an EÜR needs income, expenses, receipts and the Anlage EÜR, not account numbers, a journal or open items. Simple books show only that. The owner works on two pages: Einnahmen lists the issued invoices and records a customer's payment, and Ausgaben lists the receipts. Each ledger account is offered by name under the EÜR line it counts on, without its number. A receipt line asks for a gross amount (and a VAT rate if the business charges VAT), not for net, rate and tax. A receipt is paid as it is booked unless the owner says otherwise. Books and open items are hidden from the owner but stay reachable, and the accountant always sees them. The overview shows the year's income, expenses and profit.

An Einzelunternehmer may run the business over a private bank account. Then only business payments are recorded, each when it is paid: a receipt paid from the private account books a private deposit, and an invoice paid into it books a private withdrawal. Those private movements count in Zeilen 106 and 107. The private account's statement is never imported, so the bank page stays hidden until a business account is set up. With a business account, the owner pays and matches through it as in full books.

Underneath, nothing changes: every receipt, payment and bank match still books balanced postings on SKR03/SKR04 accounts (ADR 0011), and the EÜR is still computed from them (ADR 0021). We rejected a single-entry cash book because it would mean a second source for the EÜR, a second set of GoBD guarantees (reversal, closing, audit), and a migration once the business outgrows it. With a view, switching between simple and full books in either direction is just a setting.

The setting `simple_books` is on by default for a small business and off otherwise, and the owner can change it either way.
