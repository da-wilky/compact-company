# Quote sections are Typst markup

A quote's sections and snippets store their body as raw Typst markup, which the template `eval`s in markup mode. Plain text parsed by the app into paragraphs and lists could never break a render, but quotes vary too much for that: they need emphasis, numbered lists, tables and links. Ordinary prose breaks Typst markup (`info@swilk.eu` becomes a reference, a lone `$` opens math), and Typst reports the error at the template's `eval`, not inside the text. So the app guards it: a lint flags the known pitfalls and offers to escape them, each section is compiled on save to find the broken paragraph, and a quote can't be sent while a section fails. Every template's `quote.typ` must `eval` section bodies, and stored bodies are Typst, so switching to another format later means migrating them.
