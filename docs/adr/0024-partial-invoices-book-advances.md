# Partial invoices book advances; the final invoice books the work

A partial invoice is booked when issued, like any invoice, but against advances received (an account per VAT rate, SKR04 3272/3260/3280, SKR03 1718/1711/1719), not revenue, and its VAT as not yet due under every taxation: the VAT on an advance arises when the money arrives (§ 13 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 a Satz 4 UStG), so its payment moves the paid share to VAT owed, under the accrual basis too. The final invoice books the whole work in revenue and VAT and clears what it deducts from the advances and the VAT, leaving a receivable of only the remainder. Revenue and VAT are booked once, and the VAT return counts an advance's base when it is paid under either basis.

The final invoice deducts only paid partial invoices, as § 14 Abs. 5 UStG asks for amounts received. Issuing it cancels each unpaid one with a cancellation invoice, refuses while one is paid only in part, and records which it took in; those and the final invoice can no longer be cancelled, since undoing one alone would leave advances or VAT behind.

The advances accounts count on Zeile 15 of the Anlage EÜR (12 for a small business), so the EÜR counts an advance when it is paid and the final invoice's payment counts only the rest.

Considered: booking a partial invoice only when paid, which matches the law most directly but leaves an issued invoice without an open item and needs a second path for its payment; and deducting invoiced rather than paid amounts, which the law does not ask for and which would turn unpaid advances into a final invoice's open item.
