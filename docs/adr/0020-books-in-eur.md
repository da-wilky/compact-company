# The books are kept in EUR; other currencies at a rate the owner enters

A document or receipt in another currency is booked in EUR at an exchange rate the owner enters when issuing or booking it, and each of its payments at the EUR amount that actually arrived or left; the difference between the two posts to exchange gains or losses. The owner knows the rate the bank applied, so fetching reference rates (ECB) would add a network dependency and still not match the statement. Refusing other currencies once books are kept would drop invoicing in CHF or USD, which Phase 2 already supports.
