# Billing addresses sit under the customer's name

A customer has several billing addresses and one of them is the default; a draft or recurring invoice names one or follows the default, and issuing freezes it into the document's data as before (ADR 0019). An address has no name of its own: the customer's name heads every document and is the buyer name of the e-invoice, because it names the Leistungsempfänger. A document for another legal person needs another customer. A c/o, department or attention line goes into the street, which takes up to three lines because the EN 16931 address carries `LineOne` to `LineThree` and forbids `CareOf`, `AttentionOf`, `StreetName` and `BuildingNumber` (docs/legal-sources.md).

An address an issued document names is archived when removed, not deleted, so the document still points at it; a draft or recurring invoice that named it falls back to the default, and a follow-up of the issued document does not carry it over. We rejected editing the address on the draft itself: it would keep addresses in two places and a dunning letter could not find the invoice's address.
