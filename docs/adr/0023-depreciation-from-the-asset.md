# Depreciation is computed from the asset and booked per finished year

A fixed asset stores what it cost, when it was acquired, its method and useful life, and when it left; its schedule is computed from those, never stored. Accumulated depreciation through a month is the cost times the months used over the months of its life, rounded, so the yearly parts sum to the cost to the cent. The month acquired counts in full (§ 7 Abs. 1 Satz 4 EStG), and so does the month it leaves.

The owner books a finished year's depreciation, one posting per asset on 31 December, and a year can't be closed while any is unbooked. A disposal books that year's depreciation and the remaining book value on its day; if the year was booked already, its postings are reversed first. Asset postings are otherwise never reversed by hand, and an asset with postings only changes its name. The Anlage AVEÜR is computed from the same schedules, so it matches the asset accounts once everything is booked.

Considered: posting depreciation monthly or when the asset is registered, which shows it in the EÜR earlier but has to be undone whenever a disposal or a correction changes the schedule.
